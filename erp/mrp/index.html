Bahasa Indonesia

2. MRP (Material Requirement Planning)

MRP adalah sistem digital yang merencanakan kebutuhan material produksi berdasarkan order produksi. BOM menjadi salah satu dasar MRP karena berisi material, jumlah, dan spesifikasinya.

Fabric dan Trims

  • Fabric: kain sebagai material utama garment.
  • Trims: material pendukung seperti benang, label, renda, strap, dan foam cup.

RM Specifications digunakan untuk melihat material, article, color, size, position, dan consumption. Article membedakan material, sedangkan color membedakan variasinya.

Consumption menunjukkan kebutuhan material untuk satu unit. Contoh: consumption 0.1338 yard/pcs dan order quantity 1.540 pcs, maka kebutuhan material adalah 1.540 x 0.1338 = 206.052 yard. Consumption dapat berbeda berdasarkan size.

Langkah menu

  1. Buka menu MRP.
  2. Klik Specifications.
  3. Pilih View.
  4. Tekan F8 untuk filter berdasarkan SPI atau nomor PO.
  5. Pilih SPI, lalu pilih Fabric atau Trims.
English

2. MRP (Material Requirement Planning)

MRP is a digital system that plans production material requirements based on production orders. BOM is one of the foundations of MRP because it lists the required materials, quantities, and specifications.

Fabric and Trims

  • Fabric: the main garment material.
  • Trims: supporting materials such as thread, labels, lace, straps, and foam cups.

RM Specifications displays material, article, color, size, position, and consumption. Article identifies the material, while color identifies its variation.

Consumption is the material needed for one unit. For 0.1338 yard/pcs and 1,540 pcs, the requirement is 1,540 x 0.1338 = 206.052 yards. Consumption may vary by size.

Menu steps

  1. Open MRP, click Specifications, then choose View.
  2. Press F8 to filter by SPI or PO number.
  3. Select the SPI and choose Fabric or Trims.
